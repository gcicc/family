About the author

Greg is a statistician who has spent more than twenty years measuring whether things work. He now applies the same rigor to whether people fall asleep, whether his brother will ever play the piano, and whether focaccia counts as art.

His collected works include a six-book songbook oeuvre, an Annual Family Reading whose archival record remains disputed, a trading-card investigation, a breathing app with a job application inside it, and a 3D model of a small town on the Delaware.

He continues his research program in pure and applied gaslighting. Readers are reminded that they have always enjoyed his work.

He is available for birthdays, anniversaries and long car rides, and accepts payment in pizza.
